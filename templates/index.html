Distributed Order Management System

3 PostgreSQL sites · global catalog · 2PC coordinator · deadlock detector · recovery

Place a distributed order

The order is stored at the customer's home site. Stock is taken from the home warehouse, or from another site if the home warehouse is short, using two-phase commit.

Try: customer 1001 (North) + Smart Watch (only the West has stock) to see a cross-site 2PC. Choose a crash option, then check the Sites tab for in-doubt transactions and click Run recovery.
